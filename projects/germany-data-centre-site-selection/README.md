# A Spatial Decision Support System for Sustainable and Climate-Resilient Data Centre Site Selection in Germany

**MSc Thesis Research | University of Bonn, Germany**

**Research areas:** GIS-MCDA · Spatial Decision Support Systems · Climate Resilience · Environmental Sustainability · Geospatial Analysis

## Overview

Data centres are essential to the digital economy, but their location influences energy demand, water requirements, environmental impacts, operating conditions and exposure to climate-related risks.

This MSc thesis aims to develop an expert-informed spatial decision support system for identifying relatively suitable areas for sustainable and climate-resilient data-centre development in Germany.

The research integrates sustainability, climate resilience, infrastructure provision, resource availability, and spatial and planning compatibility into a coherent GIS-based site-selection framework.

## Research Problem

Existing data-centre siting studies address infrastructure, environmental conditions, climate risks, resource availability and land-use constraints through different frameworks and geographic contexts.

This research addresses the need for a coherent framework that systematically integrates these requirements for data-centre site selection in Germany.

## Aim

To develop and spatially operationalise an expert-informed spatial decision support system for identifying suitable locations for sustainable and climate-resilient data-centre development in Germany.

## Research Objectives

1. Synthesise relevant literature to identify siting dimensions, criteria and spatial indicators.
2. Validate and refine the proposed framework through expert assessment.
3. Determine subjective criterion weights using an appropriate expert-based multi-criteria decision analysis method, with the Best-Worst Method (BWM) considered as a candidate.
4. Implement the validated framework using appropriate spatial datasets for Germany within a GIS-MCDA environment.
5. Assess the sensitivity and robustness of the resulting suitability patterns to plausible variations in criterion weights.

## Proposed Methodology

The study follows a sequential research workflow:

1. **Literature synthesis:** Identify and organise relevant dimensions, criteria and spatial indicators.
2. **Framework development:** Establish a preliminary hierarchy of criteria and distinguish weighted suitability factors from hard exclusion constraints.
3. **Expert validation:** Refine the framework through assessment by experts in relevant fields.
4. **Criterion weighting:** Derive subjective weights using an appropriate expert-based method.
5. **GIS-MCDA implementation:** Operationalise the validated criteria using suitable datasets for Germany and integrate the weighted factors spatially.
6. **Sensitivity and robustness analysis:** Test how plausible changes in criterion weights affect the resulting suitability patterns.

Criteria will be selected according to conceptual relevance and empirical support, rather than data availability alone.

## Key Research Dimensions

The proposed framework considers five broad dimensions:

- **Environmental sustainability:** Environmental performance and ecological considerations.
- **Climate resilience:** Exposure to relevant climate hazards and climate-related operational risks.
- **Infrastructure provision:** Access to electricity, telecommunications, transport and utility networks.
- **Resource availability:** Long-term availability and sustainability of resources such as energy and water.
- **Spatial and planning compatibility:** Land-use suitability, protected areas, environmental restrictions and regulatory requirements.

The final criteria and indicators will be refined through the research process and expert assessment.

## Expected Contribution

The research aims to provide a literature-grounded and expert-informed framework for data-centre site selection in Germany. It will integrate diverse siting requirements, translate them into measurable spatial criteria, distinguish weighted factors from exclusion constraints, and evaluate the robustness of the resulting suitability patterns.

## Tools and Methods

**Planned analytical approach**

- Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)
- Spatial data integration and standardisation
- Expert-based criterion weighting
- Sensitivity and robustness analysis

Specific software, datasets and weighting methods will be documented as they are selected and implemented.
