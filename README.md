# GIS & Remote Sensing Portfolio

**GIS | Remote Sensing | Spatial Analysis | Spatial Decision Support**

Welcome to my geospatial portfolio. I am an MSc Geodetic Engineering student at the University of Bonn, Germany, with interests in GIS, satellite remote sensing, spatial modelling, and environmental applications of geospatial technology.

This portfolio showcases academic research and practical projects in environmental assessment, sustainable infrastructure planning, spatial decision support, and GIS application development.

## Featured Projects

### 1. Sustainable and Climate-Resilient Data Centre Site Selection in Germany

**MSc Thesis Research | University of Bonn**

Developing an expert-informed spatial decision support system to identify suitable locations for sustainable and climate-resilient data-centre development in Germany.

**Key methods:** GIS-based multi-criteria decision analysis (GIS-MCDA), spatial suitability modelling, expert assessment, criterion weighting, and sensitivity analysis.

**Status:** Ongoing research.

[View Thesis Project on GitHub](https://github.com/Kissi77/gis-remote-sensing-portfolio/tree/main/projects/germany-data-centre-site-selection)

### 2. Environmental Impacts of Data-Centre Development in Malaysia

**ASA Engagement Exchange Research Project**

Investigating land surface temperature, vegetation, and built-up characteristics around data-centre developments in Malaysia using satellite remote sensing and geospatial analysis.

**Key methods:** Google Earth Engine, Landsat imagery, land surface temperature (LST), Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), Normalized Difference Built-up Index (NDBI), Python, R, distance-based analysis, and impact–control assessment.

[View Research Project on GitHub](https://github.com/Kissi77/gis-remote-sensing-portfolio/tree/main/projects/malaysia-data-centre-analysis)

### 3. Bonn Alternative Route Finder

**Python | GIS Application Development**

An interactive route-planning application for exploring alternative routes and comparing travel distances through spatial network analysis.

**Key methods:** Python, network analysis, route optimisation, and interactive mapping.

[View Project on GitHub](https://github.com/Kissi77/gis-remote-sensing-portfolio/tree/main/projects/bonn-alternative-route-finder) · [Launch the Live Application](https://gis-remote-sensing-portfolio-husfpsxfrr6klhchenfr2z.streamlit.app/)

### 4. Residential Development Suitability Analysis in Meckenheim, Germany

**GIS | Spatial Planning**

Analysing land-use and forest-area change and identifying potential residential development locations using spatial constraints and accessibility criteria.

**Key methods:** Land-use analysis, spatial buffers, accessibility modelling, multi-criteria suitability assessment, and thematic cartography.

[View Project on GitHub](https://github.com/Kissi77/gis-remote-sensing-portfolio/tree/main/projects/rhein-sieg-meckenheim-gis-analysis)
